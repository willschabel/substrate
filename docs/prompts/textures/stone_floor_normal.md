# stone_floor_normal

Normal maps are derived from the albedo — do not generate directly from a text prompt.

## Workflow
1. Generate `stone_floor_albedo.png` first
2. Upload to https://cpetry.github.io/NormalMap-Online/
3. Strength: 2–4 (shallower than wall — worn smooth in centre, cracked at edges)
4. Export as PNG

## Notes
- Use lower strength than wall — floor is worn flatter by foot traffic
- Crack edges should still read but slab surfaces should be nearly flat
- Circuit trace grooves should be very shallow
