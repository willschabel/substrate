# Setting & Aesthetic

## The Core Idea

The world is an infinite 3D grid — a structure of unknown origin that predates all living memory. At some point in the distant past, it was built by an incredibly advanced civilisation. Nobody alive knows why. Nobody knows how. The technology still functions perfectly; the knowledge of it is gone.

Players are inhabitants of this structure. They were born here. Their parents were born here. The grid has always existed, and the question of *why* is treated the way we treat gravity — it simply is. A generation of explorers and scholars has begun to suspect there is an answer buried somewhere in the structure, but uncovering it is the work of lifetimes.

This is the tension at the heart of the game: **ancient technology so advanced it appears magical, operated by people who treat it as mundane infrastructure.**

---

## Visual Aesthetic: Worn Awe — Forgotten Technology

**Not** dark or grimdark. **Not** clean and pristine.

The feeling is **wonder through age** — ancient, worn, and still breathtakingly beautiful. Things are cracked but still glowing through the cracks. Machinery is tarnished but still humming. Symbols are faded but still faintly luminous. The world has lived a very long time and it shows — but that age is beautiful, not oppressive.

The correct references are:
- *The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild* — ancient Sheikah technology worn and weathered but still luminous, ruins that feel lived-in by time, nature and age coexisting beautifully
- *Moonlighter* — colorful, warm, charming adventure aesthetic, worn dungeons that feel exciting not threatening
- *Horizon Zero Dawn* — ancient machines patinated and aged but still functional and beautiful, lush world slowly reclaiming forgotten things

### The Grid Itself
The grid (white room, blue lines) is the **one exception** — it has not aged at all. It remains pristine, geometric, and perfect. This contrast is intentional: everything inside the grid shows centuries of wear, while the grid itself is eternal and untouched. That contrast is part of the mystery.

### Everything Within the Grid
Has aged gracefully. Worn but not broken. Faded but still glowing. Nature and time have worked on everything, and the result is beautiful rather than grim.

- Cracked stone walls with warm light bleeding through the fissures
- Worn circuit traces still pulsing gold and teal beneath layers of age
- Tarnished ancient machinery still humming, draped in moss and flowering vines
- Symbols faded but still faintly luminous — intriguing, not threatening
- Weathered armour and weapons that incorporate ancient components still softly glowing
- Light bleeding through cracks and gaps in aged stone, warm and inviting

### Colour Palette
- **Structure:** weathered sandstone, tarnished bronze, worn grey stone, aged iron
- **Ancient tech:** faintly glowing blue-white circuitry, cold teal tech glow, holographic symbols flickering — the same hue as the grid, hinting at a connection
- **Nature:** vibrant greens, wildflower colours, warm amber sunlight
- **Patina & age:** verdigris, oxidised copper, warm rust tones, worn leather
- **Lived-in warmth:** warm amber candlelight contrasting cold teal tech glow — this contrast is the signature of every inhabited space
- **Contrast:** cool blue tech glow against warm human habitation — and the eternal pristine grid against everything inside it showing centuries of wear

### What This Means for Each Asset

| Asset | Direction |
|---|---|
| Player base interior | Cracked ancient chamber repurposed as a living space, worn stone with light bleeding through fissures, aged circuit traces still glowing, moss and plants through weathered floors |
| Grid (exterior, through windows) | Pristine, luminous, eternal — the only clean thing in the world, framed by worn and cracked stone |
| Combat locations (indoor) | Weathered ancient halls, worn circuit murals still faintly glowing, tarnished machinery still turning, luminous moss in cracked floors |
| Combat locations (outdoor) | Rolling hills with ancient weathered stones half-buried, vibrant nature, worn circuit traces through cracked rock, grid as glowing blue sky |
| Weapons | Aged and tarnished but still beautiful — worn leather, oxidised metal, ancient power cells still faintly glowing through patina |
| Enemies | Ancient autonomous constructs — tarnished and aged but still functioning, worn symbols still faintly luminous, beautiful despite their age |
| Player character | Someone who grew up here. Practical worn clothing, aged armour incorporating ancient components still softly glowing |

## The Mystery

The central unanswered question — *why does the grid exist and who built it* — should inspire wonder, not dread. Symbols that appear meaningless have a pattern. Machinery that seems decorative occasionally does something unexpected. The grid itself, when viewed from certain angles or under certain conditions, reveals structure that shouldn't be there.

The answer, when players find pieces of it, should recontextualise everything they've seen. Build the world so that answer is always consistent with what already exists.

---

## Lore Constraints for Development

- The grid predates all written history in the world
- No NPC knows its origin — scholars theorise, none know
- The tech still functions but is not fully understood by anyone
- "Magic" in this world is almost always misunderstood ancient technology
- The player's base navigation system (the elevator controller) is one of these ancient tools — players use it because it works, not because they understand it
